The Future of Work is Here...

A cross disciplinary, empirical research hub that disseminates and conducts new research services that addresses how our labor markets, organizations and policymakers better create work that is sustainable for all our human resources

Document how work, jobs, technological advance and the economy promote (or harm) long term health and well-being while still improve the performance of workers, firms, communities and labor markets? What minimum and maximum quantity of work can sustain the financial and mental wellbeing of individuals? What minimum quality of jobs and work can provide opportunities for flourishing? Do current labor market trends signal sustainability? How can the research inform any evidence-based policy discussion to achieve labor sustainability?

The Five Pillars

  1. Sustainable Hours of Work, Workforce and Workplaces (SHOWWW) Analysis and Practices – Goldielocks Hours
  2. Health and Well-being-Creating Work and Workplaces
  3. Job Quality Measurement and Improvement
  4. Labor Market Analysis, Current and Future Trends and Patterns
  5. Labor Standards, Legal and Policy Analysis
