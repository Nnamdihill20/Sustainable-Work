About the Sustainable Work Lab

The Sustainable Work Lab is an interdisciplinary research lab at Penn State Abington. It studies job quality, sustainable working time and worker well-being, and how work can be designed to keep the people doing it healthy.

The lab shares its research with students, scholars, policymakers, journalists and community organizations. If you would like to collaborate, you are welcome to get in touch.
